# Viral Hook Creator

The hook decides whether the rest of the content exists. Generate hooks that earn the next second of attention without lying about what follows.

## When to use

- "Write hooks for...", "give me opening lines", "title ideas", "my posts get no engagement"
- Repurposing one piece of content into many angles

## Process

### 1. Establish inputs

You need three things (ask only for what's missing):
- **The core content/claim**: what is the post/video actually about?
- **The platform**: hooks are formatted differently per platform (see below)
- **The audience**: who must stop scrolling, and what do they want or fear?

### 2. Find the tension

A hook works when it creates an open loop the reader must close. Before writing, identify:
- What's **surprising or counterintuitive** in the content?
- What **mistake** does the audience make that this addresses?
- What **result** does it promise, and can it be made specific (numbers, timeframe)?
- What **enemy** does it name (a common practice, belief, or tool)?

### 3. Generate using proven patterns

Produce 10-15 hooks across DIFFERENT patterns (don't give 10 variants of one pattern):

- **Contrarian**: "Everything you've heard about [X] is backwards."
- **Specific result**: "We went from 0 to 4,200 subscribers in 60 days. Here's the exact system."
- **Mistake callout**: "You're losing customers in the first 5 seconds of your demo. Here's where."
- **Curiosity gap**: "The pricing change that doubled our revenue took 20 minutes to ship."
- **Direct address / filter**: "If you're a founder doing your own sales, stop doing this one thing."
- **Confession / vulnerability**: "I wasted $30k on ads before I understood this."
- **Listicle promise**: "7 cold email openers that got replies from CEOs (with real examples)."
- **Question that stings**: "When did you last talk to a user who churned?"
- **Before/after snapshot**: "Same product. Same price. New headline. 3x the signups."
- **Authority borrow**: "I studied 100 Product Hunt #1 launches. They share 4 things."

### 4. Format for the platform

- **X/Twitter**: first line stands alone; under ~60 chars hits hardest; no hashtags in the hook
- **LinkedIn**: first 1-2 lines show before "...see more" — the cut is the hook
- **Short video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)**: spoken line for seconds 0-3 + on-screen text version
- **YouTube**: title (<60 chars) + thumbnail text (<5 words) as a pair — they must not repeat each other
- **Email**: subject line (<50 chars) + preview text as a pair

### 5. Deliver

- Present hooks grouped by pattern, with a one-line note on when each pattern fits
- Mark your top 3 picks and say why
- Offer to write the full post/script for whichever hook the user picks

## Rules

- **Never write a hook the content can't cash.** Curiosity gap ≠ bait-and-switch; the payoff must exist.
- Specific beats clever: numbers, names, timeframes.
- Cut throat-clearing: the hook is the first word, not the third sentence.
- Match the user's actual voice — if they've shared past posts, mirror their tone.
