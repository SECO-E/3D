# Lead Magnet Generator

Design a lead magnet people actually want, then create it. A good lead magnet solves ONE narrow, urgent problem in under 10 minutes of the user's time — it's a painkiller sample, not a textbook.

## When to use

- "I need a lead magnet", "how do I grow my email list", "what should I gate"
- The user has traffic but no email capture, or a newsletter with weak signup incentive

## Process

### 1. Ground it in the business

Establish (ask only what's missing):
- What does the business sell, and to whom?
- What's the #1 problem the ideal customer is actively trying to solve *right before* they'd buy?
- Where will the magnet be promoted (site, social, ads, in-content)?

**The golden rule**: the lead magnet must attract *buyers*, not freebie-seekers. It should be a fast win on the same problem the paid product solves fully.

### 2. Pick the format

Recommend 2-3 concepts, each scored on: desirability, speed-to-consume, effort-to-create, and lead quality. Formats ranked roughly by conversion power:

1. **Templates & swipe files** — "steal this" beats "learn this" (email scripts, notion templates, contract templates)
2. **Checklists & cheat sheets** — one page, immediately actionable
3. **Calculators / scorecards / quizzes** — interactive, personalized output, high perceived value
4. **Mini-guides / playbooks** — only when the topic genuinely needs depth; keep under 15 pages
5. **Free tools / audits** — highest value, highest effort
6. Avoid by default: generic ebooks, "ultimate guides" nobody reads, webinar replays

### 3. Create it

Once the user picks a concept, **actually produce the content**, not an outline:
- Write the full checklist/template/guide in markdown (or generate the file)
- For calculators/tools: build it (HTML page or spreadsheet formula structure)
- Include the branding hooks: title, subtitle, a short intro connecting the problem to the user's paid offering, and a soft CTA at the end ("If you want X done for you / the full system → [product]")

### 4. Package the funnel around it

Deliver alongside the magnet:
- **Title options** (3-5): specific outcome + timeframe/number ("The 12-Point Pre-Launch Checklist" beats "Launch Guide")
- **Landing page copy**: headline, 3 bullets (each = an outcome, not a feature of the PDF), CTA button text, and the single form field to ask for (email only, usually)
- **Delivery email**: subject + body delivering the asset and setting up the next email
- **3-email follow-up sketch**: value → case/story → pitch

## Rules

- Narrow wins: "Cold email templates for agency founders" beats "Marketing guide".
- The magnet must be consumable in one sitting and produce a visible result.
- Never propose a magnet disconnected from the paid product — leads must be pre-qualified by wanting it.
- Deliver finished assets, not homework for the user.
