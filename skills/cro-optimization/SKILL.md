# CRO Optimization

Systematically find and fix conversion leaks. The goal is a prioritized list of changes with reasoning — not generic advice like "improve your CTA".

## When to use

- "Review my landing page", "why isn't my page converting", "CRO audit"
- Low signup/trial/demo/purchase rates despite decent traffic
- Redesigning a page and wanting conversion-first structure

## Process

### 1. Gather context

Before auditing, establish:
- **The page/flow**: URL, screenshot, or pasted copy. If a URL is given and web access is available, fetch it.
- **The single conversion goal** of the page (one page = one goal)
- **Traffic source(s)**: cold ads, SEO, social, referrals — intent differs wildly
- **Current numbers** if known: traffic, conversion rate, where people drop off
- **The audience**: who lands here and what do they already know/believe?

### 2. Audit against the conversion framework

Evaluate in this order — fix upstream problems first:

**A. Message match & clarity (biggest lever)**
- Can a stranger say what this is, who it's for, and why it's better within 5 seconds?
- Does the headline match the promise of the ad/post/link that brought them here?
- Is it about the visitor's outcome, or about the product's features?

**B. Offer strength**
- Is the call to action proportionate to trust earned? (Cold traffic rarely "books a call"; they might grab a free tool)
- Is there risk reversal: free trial, money-back, no credit card, cancel anytime?

**C. Friction**
- Form fields: every field costs conversions — which can be removed or deferred?
- Steps in the flow: can any be merged or eliminated?
- Page speed, mobile layout, popups interrupting reading

**D. Anxiety & trust**
- Social proof: specific and relevant (numbers, names, logos, results) beats generic stars
- Objection handling: pricing clarity, security, "what happens after I click?"

**E. Attention & hierarchy**
- One primary CTA, visually dominant, repeated at natural decision points
- Does anything compete with or distract from the goal (nav links, multiple offers)?

### 3. Deliver the audit

Structure output as:

1. **Diagnosis** — the 1-3 core problems, in plain language, with evidence from the page
2. **Prioritized fixes** — table with: change, why it will help, effort (S/M/L), expected impact (high/med/low). Order by impact-to-effort.
3. **Rewritten copy** — actually rewrite the headline, subhead, and CTA. Show before → after. Give 2-3 headline variants.
4. **Test plan** — which changes to A/B test vs. just ship (small-traffic sites should ship obvious fixes, not test everything)

## Rules

- Never give advice without tying it to THIS page and THIS audience.
- Rewrite copy instead of saying "make the copy better".
- Respect traffic reality: below ~1,000 conversions/month, most A/B tests won't reach significance — say so and recommend sequential shipping.
- If the real problem is the offer or the traffic, say that directly rather than polishing the page.
