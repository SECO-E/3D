# Marketing Ideas

Generate marketing ideas that fit THIS business — its stage, audience, budget, and founder strengths — and rank them so the user knows what to do Monday morning. The output is a shortlist of scoped experiments, not a firehose of tactics.

## When to use

- "Give me marketing ideas", "how do I get more customers", "what should I try next"
- Stalled growth, new channel exploration, campaign brainstorms for a launch/season

## Process

### 1. Ground in the business

Establish (one batch of questions, only what's missing):
- Product, price point, and ICP — who buys and where do they hang out?
- What's been tried: what worked (even a little), what flopped?
- Assets & constraints: budget/month, hours/week available, existing audience/list/community, founder's strengths (writing? video? talking? code?)
- Goal & horizon: leads this month vs. brand this year

### 2. Generate across the full map

Produce 15-25 ideas spanning categories — don't cluster in one:

- **Founder-led content**: X/LinkedIn insights, build-in-public, teardown posts, niche YouTube/podcast
- **Outbound**: cold email/DM plays, "audit/teardown as opener" offers, unbundled consulting
- **Community & borrowed audiences**: niche communities, podcast guesting, newsletter sponsorships/swaps, guest posts, partner webinars
- **SEO & programmatic**: comparison pages ("X vs Y"), template/tool libraries, programmatic pages on a data asset
- **Engineering-as-marketing**: free calculators, graders, mini-tools, open-source, public data reports
- **Product-led loops**: referral incentives, "powered by" badges, shareable outputs, waitlist mechanics
- **Partnerships & integrations**: marketplace listings, integration co-launches, affiliate/reseller motions
- **Paid** (only with proven conversion path): niche newsletters, retargeting, search on high-intent terms
- **Events & stunts**: challenges, live teardowns, awards/rankings for the niche, contrarian PR angles
- **Retention-as-growth**: win-back campaigns, case-study engine, testimonial harvesting, referral asks at success moments

Each idea gets 2-3 sentences: WHAT specifically to do, WHY it fits this business, and the first step. "Do SEO" is banned; "publish 5 '[Competitor] alternative' comparison pages targeting the switchers already searching" is the standard.

### 3. Prioritize ruthlessly

Score the list ICE-style (Impact, Confidence, Ease — 1-5 each) in a table, then recommend:
- **Top 3 to start now**, each expanded into a mini-brief: goal metric, exact first actions for week 1, weekly time/cost, and a 2-4 week kill/scale criterion
- **The bench**: next 5, one line each, with what would trigger promoting them
- **Explicitly skip**: 2-3 popular tactics that DON'T fit this business, with reasons — saves the user from FOMO

### 4. Match to the founder

Weight recommendations by founder strengths and honest capacity: a great writer with 5 hrs/week gets a content+outbound plan, not a YouTube channel. Never prescribe more than ~3 concurrent experiments.

## Rules

- Every idea names a specific action, audience, and channel — no category-level advice.
- Respect the budget: don't propose paid ads to a $0-budget bootstrapper.
- Reuse what worked: past signal beats novel tactics; double down before diversifying.
- Include kill criteria — ideas without an exit condition become zombie projects.
- No growth-hack spam, fake scarcity, review manipulation, or platform-TOS violations.
