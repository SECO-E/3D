# Go-to-Market Plan

Produce a GTM plan that answers, concretely: who buys this, why now, through which channel, with what message, and how we'll know it's working. Bias toward the first 90 days and the first 100 customers — not a 40-page strategy deck.

## When to use

- "How do I launch this", "GTM plan for...", "how do I get my first customers"
- Entering a new segment/geography, launching a major feature or pricing tier

## Process

### 1. Establish the foundation

Gather (ask only what's missing, in one batch):
- **Product**: what it does, state of readiness, pricing (or hypothesis)
- **ICP**: who exactly is the first target customer? Push for narrow: role + company type + situation ("RevOps lead at 20-100 person B2B SaaS running HubSpot")
- **Problem & alternative**: what do they do today instead? (The competitor is usually a spreadsheet or "nothing")
- **Assets**: audience, email list, network, existing customers, budget, founder's time available
- **Motion hypothesis**: self-serve, sales-led, community/PLG, partner-led?

### 2. Positioning first

Before channels, nail the message. Produce:
- **Positioning statement**: For [ICP] who [struggle/situation], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiator].
- **The "why now"**: what makes this urgent for the buyer today?
- **3 core messages**, each mapped to an ICP pain, each with proof (feature, number, or story)

### 3. Channel selection

Score candidate channels against: (a) where the ICP already pays attention, (b) founder's unfair advantages, (c) time-to-signal, (d) cost. Pick **1 primary + 1 secondary** — not five.

Typical early-stage menu: founder-led outbound, founder-led content (X/LinkedIn), communities where ICP lives, SEO (slow — flag it), partnerships/integrations, marketplaces/directories, paid (only with proven conversion), launch platforms (Product Hunt, HN).

Be honest about sequencing: most B2B products' first 10 customers come from network + outbound, not inbound.

### 4. The 90-day plan

```markdown
# GTM Plan — [Product]

## ICP & positioning
[from steps above]

## Motion & pricing
How people buy, what they pay, free trial/demo/pilot mechanics.

## Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Validation
Goal: [N] conversations / [N] paying users. Exact activities and daily/weekly volume
(e.g. "20 outbound messages/day to [ICP] with [message]").

## Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Repeatability
Double down on what got responses; kill what didn't. Launch event if applicable.

## Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): Scale the working channel
Systemize: templates, cadences, content calendar, first hire/contractor if justified.

## Metrics & kill criteria
Weekly funnel numbers to track + "if [metric] < [threshold] by [date], we revisit [assumption]".
```

### 5. Make it executable

Include ready-to-use assets for the primary channel: the outreach message, the launch post, or the first 5 content topics — whichever matches the chosen channel.

## Rules

- Narrow ICP beats broad. If the user resists narrowing, pick a beachhead and note expansion comes later.
- One primary channel. Diversification is for after signal.
- Every phase has a numeric goal and a date.
- Flag hard truths: no differentiated positioning → the plan is fixing that, not picking channels.
