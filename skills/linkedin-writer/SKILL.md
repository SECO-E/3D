# LinkedIn Writer

Write LinkedIn content that positions the author as a credible operator and quietly generates pipeline — without the cringe that plagues the platform.

## When to use

- "Write a LinkedIn post about...", "help me post consistently on LinkedIn", "announce X on LinkedIn"
- Founder brand building, hiring posts, launch/funding announcements, thought leadership

## Platform mechanics that shape the writing

- Only the first ~2 lines show before "...see more" — they are the hook and must create an open loop.
- Dwell time drives distribution: longer posts that keep people reading outperform one-liners.
- External links depress reach — put links in the comments or at the very end, and say so.
- Format for skimming: 1-2 sentence paragraphs, line breaks between every thought, occasional lists.
- Comments within the first hour matter; end with something worth replying to (a real question, not "Agree?").

## Post archetypes (pick per goal)

1. **Story → lesson** (authority + reach): a specific professional moment, told with tension, landing on a transferable insight. The workhorse format.
2. **Contrarian take** (reach): challenge a common practice in the niche, argue it with evidence, invite pushback.
3. **Playbook/how-to** (saves + follows): "How we did X: [numbered steps]". Specific numbers and tools.
4. **Case study/result** (pipeline): client or company result with the before/after and the method. Soft CTA.
5. **Announcement** (launch/funding/hiring): lead with what it means for the reader/market, not "I'm thrilled".
6. **Personal/vulnerable** (connection): failures, doubts, transitions. Use sparingly and only true stories.

## Process

1. **Gather**: the raw story/insight/news, the author's role and audience, and the goal (reach, authority, leads, hires). Ask only for what's missing.
2. **Find the story**: LinkedIn rewards narrative. Even a how-to lands harder opened with the moment it was learned. Push the user for specifics: numbers, dialogue, dates, what almost went wrong.
3. **Draft** with this skeleton:
   - **Hook (lines 1-2)**: tension, surprising claim, or specific result. No context-setting first.
   - **Story/body**: short paragraphs, chronological or numbered, concrete details.
   - **The turn**: the lesson or takeaway, stated plainly.
   - **Close**: one question or one soft CTA. Never both, never three hashtags of desperation (0-3 niche hashtags max, or none).
4. **De-cringe pass** — delete or rewrite: "I'm humbled/thrilled to announce", "Let that sink in", "Agree?", fake-deep one-liners stacked for effect, humble-brags disguised as gratitude, engagement-bait polls with obvious answers, AI-tell phrases ("In today's fast-paced world", "game-changer").
5. Deliver **2-3 variants** (e.g. story-led vs. list-led) and note which archetype each is.

## Comment & engagement strategy (when asked about growth)

- 30 min/day: substantive comments (2-4 sentences with an added insight) on 5-10 posts by people the target audience follows — this outperforms posting more.
- Posting cadence: 2-4×/week beats daily filler.
- Profile as landing page: headline = who you help + how; featured section = lead magnet or booking link.

## Rules

- True stories only — never invent anecdotes, clients, or numbers; extract real ones from the user.
- Write like the author talks; strip corporate diction.
- One post, one message. If two lessons emerge, that's two posts (say so — it fills the calendar).
- Announcements answer "so what?" for the reader within the first three lines.
