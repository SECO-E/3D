# Product Hunt Launch Plan

Build a complete Product Hunt launch operation: preparation timeline, every asset written, and an hour-by-hour launch day playbook. PH launches are won in the 3 weeks before launch day — the plan reflects that.

## When to use

- "Help me launch on Product Hunt", "PH launch checklist", "write my PH tagline/first comment"

## What matters on PH (ground truth for the plan)

- Ranking is driven by votes/engagement from **established PH accounts**, weighted early — the first 4 hours set trajectory. New/burner accounts are discounted and mass vote-begging can hurt.
- Launch runs midnight–midnight **Pacific time**; going live at 12:01 AM PT maximizes the voting window.
- Tuesday–Thursday have the most traffic (and competition); weekends are easier to rank but yield less traffic. Recommend based on the user's goal: badge vs. reach.
- Comments and maker responsiveness matter; the algorithm and humans both notice.
- Real outcome to optimize: email signups/trials and the launch's content byproducts — not just the badge.

## Process

### 1. Scope the launch

Ask: product + one-line pitch, launch goal (users? badge? investors watching?), audience assets on hand (email list, X/LinkedIn following, communities, teammates), and target date flexibility.

### 2. Build the plan

**T-minus 3 weeks — Foundation**
- Set date (recommend one, per goals above); create/warm PH accounts (maker profile complete, engage genuinely on PH daily)
- "Coming soon" teaser page on PH to collect followers
- Start building the **notify list**: everyone who'll be pinged on launch day, grouped by channel (DMs, email, communities, team)

**T-minus 2 weeks — Assets** (write these WITH the user; this is where the skill delivers)
- **Tagline** (60 chars): outcome-focused, no buzzwords — generate 5-8 options
- **Description** (260 chars)
- **Gallery**: first image is the billboard — outcome-headline + product shot; 4-6 more images each showing one benefit; optional 30-60s video script
- **Maker's first comment**: the story (why we built it), what's special, what's next, a question inviting feedback, plus any launch offer/promo code — draft in the maker's voice
- **Launch-day messages**: personal DM template (short, no vote-begging — "we're live, would love your feedback"), email blast, X thread + LinkedIn post, community posts (each community's rules respected)

**T-minus 1 week — Mobilization**
- Personally message the notify list to warm them ("launching Tuesday — can I ping you?"); soft-commit ≥30-50 supporters for the first hours
- Dry-run: preview listing, test links/UTMs, prep the site for PH traffic (banner + PH-specific offer)
- Schedule launch-day coverage: who's answering comments in which time zones

**Launch day — hour-by-hour**
- 12:01 AM PT: go live; maker comment posted immediately
- 12:05–2:00 AM: ping the first supporter wave (personal DMs > blasts); post to X/LinkedIn
- Morning EU / US: second and third waves, email blast, community posts
- All day: respond to EVERY comment within minutes; share milestones ("#3 and climbing") to socials; monitor for issues
- Never: buy votes, run vote-exchange threads, or link people directly with "upvote this" — link to the homepage/launch, ask for feedback

**T-plus 1 week — Harvest**
- Thank supporters publicly; badge on site; results post ("what we learned launching on PH") on X/LinkedIn
- Email new signups with onboarding sequence; tag PH cohort for tracking
- Retro: signups, activation, and what to reuse next launch

### 3. Deliver

Output: (1) the dated checklist customized to their timeline, (2) all copy assets drafted, (3) the hour-by-hour day plan, (4) the DM/email/post templates. Offer to draft the gallery-image headlines and video script next.

## Rules

- All outreach asks for *support/feedback*, never explicitly for upvotes to comply with PH norms.
- Copy assets use the product's real voice and facts — extract them, don't invent.
- Be realistic: no audience + no prep = mediocre result; say so and prioritize the notify list.
- PH is one spike, not a strategy — plan the harvest so the spike compounds.
